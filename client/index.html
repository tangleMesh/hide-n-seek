How to play

  • Hiders are invisible in the dark. Seekers only see what their flashlight shows.
  • Every step makes noise ripples that the other side can see. Shift to sneak silently, Space to sprint (loud).
  • Gravel and water are loud, carpet and grass quiet. Doors creak. Bushes hide you.
  • Standing still for over 20s makes your heartbeat pulse. Keep moving.
  • Caught hiders become seekers. Seekers win if everyone is caught, hiders win if anyone survives the timer.
  • Item pads (E/click to use) respawn on fixed timers. Seekers: right-click to mark a spot for the team.
  • Last 60s: Final Hush. Every heartbeat pulses.
